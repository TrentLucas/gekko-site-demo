Traditional human investigation

Traditional human investigation starts with an alert. The engineer then juggles between dashboards, logs, and timestamps, leaning on gut feelings and memories of past outages. The whole ordeal takes anywhere from 30 minutes to a few hours. When done, the answer usually stays in their head or gets lost in a Slack thread.

Automated AI investigation

With Gekko Probe, the moment an alert fires, it scans the full event cluster, checks timing and patterns, tests hypotheses, builds a clear evidence chain and highlights the root cause with recommendations in just minutes. All findings are saved as reusable records, so you never need to rely on memory or dig through old chats again.

The AI investigates your data. It never reads your data.

Most AI tools send raw logs to an AI provider for analysis. That can expose internal IPs, usernames, error messages, and other sensitive operational data.

Gekko Probe works differently. Your raw logs stay where they already are, inside your monitoring tools. Gekko Probe creates a compact abstract of the data inside a secure sandbox for analysis. The AI reasons from that abstract and requests additional evidence when needed. Your underlying log data never enters the AI’s context.

Other AI summarizes. Gekko Probe investigates.

Most AI tools summarize your alerts. Gekko Probe investigates them the way a senior engineer would, with three specialized agents working the incident together. The first identifies the signals that matter. The second tests the theory against the evidence. The third connects the findings and writes up what went wrong and what to do about it.

Identify the signal. Test the theory. Find the root cause.

  1. TriageAgent

    Detective

    • Reviews thousands of events
    • Identifies patterns, timing, and affected systems
    • Separates meaningful signals from noise
    • Forms an initial root cause hypothesis
    • Creates a targeted plan for deeper investigation
  2. AnalysisAgent

    Forensic Analyst

    • Reviews the initial hypothesis
    • Identifies what evidence is missing
    • Requests additional data for investigation
    • Tests the theory against the evidence
    • Confirms or challenges the root cause
  3. SynthesisAgent

    Lead Investigator

    • Connects the evidence across the incident
    • Determines the root cause and confidence level
    • Explains the sequence of events and impact
    • Recommends immediate actions and next steps
    • Delivers an escalation-ready RCA
